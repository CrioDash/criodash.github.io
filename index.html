01 / Released game

Cube Miner

August—November 2023

A mobile arcade game inspired by Fruit Ninja. The player slices blocks, descends deeper into a mine, collects coins, and uses bonuses and power-ups.

Role Solo developer
Platform Android
Built with Unity · C# · Pixel art

What I built

  • Touch-based slicing and core gameplay systems
  • Progression, bonuses, currency, and shop systems
  • Pixel-art graphics, UI, animation, and audio
  • Android build preparation and Google Play release

Developed independently from concept to release. The game was previously available on Google Play and is no longer listed.

02 / Prototype

Dungeon Break Breaker

January—February 2024

A fantasy-themed reinterpretation of Arkanoid. Instead of controlling a paddle and bouncing a ball, the player fires projectiles from a cannon at formations of enemies.

Role Solo developer
Status Gameplay prototype
Built with Unity · C# · Pixel art

What I built

  • Cannon aiming and projectile mechanics
  • Multiple enemy types with distinct properties
  • Enemy formations and large gameplay encounters
  • Pixel-art characters, environments, UI, and effects

Developed independently as an experiment in transforming a classic brick-breaker structure through direct shooting and varied enemy combinations.
